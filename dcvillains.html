Top 10 DC Villains

Home Marvel Heroes Marvel Villains DC Heroes DC Villains Application

Darkseid

Real Name: Darkseid

First Appearance: 1970

Darkseid is the tyrannical ruler of Apokolips. He seeks the Anti-Life Equation to control every living being in the universe and is one of DC's most powerful villains.

Brainiac

Real Name: Brainiac

First Appearance: 1958

Brainiac is a super-intelligent alien android who shrinks entire cities to collect their knowledge before destroying planets.

Lex Luthor

Real Name: Lex Luthor

First Appearance: 1940

Lex Luthor is a billionaire genius and Superman's greatest enemy. He relies on his intelligence, wealth and advanced technology to battle heroes.

Reverse-Flash

Real Name: Eobard Thawne

First Appearance: 1963

Reverse-Flash is the arch-enemy of The Flash. His connection to the Negative Speed Force gives him incredible speed and the ability to alter history.

Black Manta

Real Name: David Hyde

First Appearance: 1967

Black Manta is Aquaman's greatest enemy. His advanced armour and relentless hatred make him one of DC's deadliest villains.

Cheetah

Real Name: Barbara Minerva

First Appearance: 1987

Cheetah is Wonder Woman's greatest rival. She possesses incredible speed, strength and razor-sharp claws.

Sinestro

Real Name: Thaal Sinestro

First Appearance: 1961

Once Green Lantern's greatest mentor, Sinestro became one of his greatest enemies by creating the fear-powered Sinestro Corps.

Black Adam

Real Name: Teth-Adam

First Appearance: 1945

Black Adam possesses powers similar to Shazam. Although sometimes an anti-hero, he has often been one of DC's most dangerous villains.

Joker

Real Name: Unknown

First Appearance: 1940

The Joker is Batman's greatest enemy. His unpredictable mind, twisted humour and love of chaos make him one of comic books' most iconic villains.

Mongul

Real Name: Mongul

First Appearance: 1980

Mongul is the ruthless ruler of Warworld. He possesses immense strength and has challenged Superman and the Justice League many times.
