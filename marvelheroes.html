Top 10 Marvel Heroes

Spider-Man

Real Name: Peter Parker

First Appearance: 1962

Spider-Man gained his powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider. He protects New York City while living by the motto, "With great power comes great responsibility."

Iron Man

Real Name: Tony Stark

First Appearance: 1963

A billionaire inventor who created a powerful suit of armour. Iron Man is one of the founding members of the Avengers.

Captain America

Real Name: Steve Rogers

First Appearance: 1941

Steve Rogers became a super soldier during World War II and fights with his famous vibranium shield.

Thor

Real Name: Thor Odinson

First Appearance: 1962

Thor is the Asgardian God of Thunder who uses the enchanted hammer Mjolnir to defend Earth and Asgard.

Hulk

Real Name: Bruce Banner

First Appearance: 1962

Bruce Banner transforms into the Hulk whenever he becomes angry, giving him incredible strength and durability.

Wolverine

Real Name: Logan

First Appearance: 1974

Wolverine is a mutant with an incredible healing factor and razor-sharp adamantium claws.

Black Panther

Real Name: T'Challa

First Appearance: 1966

The king of Wakanda, Black Panther protects his nation using advanced technology and enhanced abilities.

Doctor Strange

Real Name: Stephen Strange

First Appearance: 1963

Once a brilliant surgeon, Doctor Strange became the Sorcerer Supreme and protects the world from magical threats.

Scarlet Witch

Real Name: Wanda Maximoff

First Appearance: 1964

Scarlet Witch possesses powerful magic and reality-warping abilities, making her one of Marvel's strongest heroes.

Captain Marvel

Real Name: Carol Danvers

First Appearance: 1968

Captain Marvel is one of the most powerful heroes in the Marvel Universe with incredible strength, speed and energy powers.
