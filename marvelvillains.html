Top 10 Marvel Villains

Thanos

Real Name: Thanos

First Appearance: 1973

Thanos is the Mad Titan who sought the Infinity Stones to wipe out half of all life in the universe and create what he believed was perfect balance.

Loki

Real Name: Loki Laufeyson

First Appearance: 1962

Loki is the God of Mischief and Thor's adopted brother. He uses magic, deception and illusion to outsmart his enemies.

Green Goblin

Real Name: Norman Osborn

First Appearance: 1964

Green Goblin is Spider-Man's greatest enemy. He rides a Goblin Glider and attacks with pumpkin bombs and advanced weapons.

Doctor Doom

Real Name: Victor Von Doom

First Appearance: 1962

Doctor Doom is a brilliant scientist and ruler of Latveria. He combines advanced technology with powerful magic.

Ultron

Real Name: Ultron

First Appearance: 1968

Ultron is an artificial intelligence that turned against humanity. He constantly evolves and creates powerful robotic armies.

Magneto

Real Name: Max Eisenhardt

First Appearance: 1963

Magneto can control magnetic fields and metal. He believes mutants are superior and often clashes with the X-Men.

Venom

Real Name: Eddie Brock

First Appearance: 1988

Venom is bonded with an alien symbiote that grants enormous strength, shape-shifting abilities and incredible healing.

Red Skull

Real Name: Johann Schmidt

First Appearance: 1941

Red Skull is Captain America's greatest enemy and a ruthless mastermind who seeks world domination.

Hela

Real Name: Hela

First Appearance: 1964

Hela is the Asgardian Goddess of Death. She possesses immense magical power and once attempted to conquer Asgard.

Kingpin

Real Name: Wilson Fisk

First Appearance: 1967

Kingpin is one of Marvel's most powerful crime bosses. Although he has no superpowers, his intelligence and strength make him a dangerous opponent.
