Top 10 DC Heroes

Batman

Real Name: Bruce Wayne

First Appearance: 1939

Batman is the protector of Gotham City. Using his intelligence, wealth and advanced gadgets, he fights crime without possessing any superpowers.

Superman

Real Name: Clark Kent

First Appearance: 1938

Superman is one of the world's greatest heroes. Born on the planet Krypton, he possesses incredible strength, flight, heat vision and super speed.

Wonder Woman

Real Name: Diana Prince

First Appearance: 1941

Wonder Woman is an Amazon warrior princess who fights for peace and justice using her strength, sword, shield and Lasso of Truth.

The Flash

Real Name: Barry Allen

First Appearance: 1956

The Flash is the fastest man alive. His connection to the Speed Force allows him to move at incredible speeds and even travel through time.

Green Lantern

Real Name: Hal Jordan

First Appearance: 1959

Green Lantern uses a powerful ring that can create almost anything he imagines. He serves as a protector of the universe.

Aquaman

Real Name: Arthur Curry

First Appearance: 1941

Aquaman is the King of Atlantis. He has superhuman strength, can communicate with sea life and rules the oceans.

Cyborg

Real Name: Victor Stone

First Appearance: 1980

Cyborg is a human enhanced with advanced technology, giving him incredible strength, powerful weapons and computer-based abilities.

Shazam

Real Name: Billy Batson

First Appearance: 1940

Billy Batson transforms into Shazam by saying the magic word "Shazam!" and gains the powers of legendary heroes.

Green Arrow

Real Name: Oliver Queen

First Appearance: 1941

Green Arrow is a billionaire vigilante and master archer. He protects Star City using his exceptional archery skills and trick arrows.

Nightwing

Real Name: Dick Grayson

First Appearance: 1984

Nightwing was the first Robin before becoming an independent hero. He is one of DC's greatest martial artists and leaders.
