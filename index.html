Marvel

Marvel Comics was founded in 1939 as Timely Comics by Martin Goodman. During the 1960s, Stan Lee, Jack Kirby and Steve Ditko introduced many iconic superheroes including Spider-Man, Iron Man, Thor, Hulk, Captain America and the X-Men.

Today, Marvel is known worldwide through the Marvel Cinematic Universe, featuring blockbuster films and unforgettable characters.

DC Comics

DC Comics was founded in 1934 and introduced legendary heroes such as Superman, Batman and Wonder Woman. It remains one of the biggest comic book companies in the world.

DC is also home to famous characters like The Flash, Green Lantern, Aquaman and Cyborg, along with villains such as The Joker, Lex Luthor and Darkseid.
